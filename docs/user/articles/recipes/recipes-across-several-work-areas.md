These playbooks show how teams combine recipes for bigger pieces of association work. They are not automation promises. They are guided journeys: gather evidence, review the plan, approve sensitive steps, then verify the outcome.

## New member from enquiry to welcome

**For:** Membership team, Member services · **Covers:** Membership, billing, portal access, communications

| Stage | Powered by | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Check the person and possible duplicates | [R-01-01](/docs/recipes/membership-lifecycle-recipes/#understand-a-member-before-deciding-what-to-do-next), [R-10-02](/docs/recipes/data-quality-and-duplicates-recipes/#review-one-possible-duplicate-pair) | One clear candidate record and any duplicate concern before onboarding. |
| 2. Prepare the join or renewal action | [R-01-02](/docs/recipes/membership-lifecycle-recipes/#onboard-a-new-individual-member-from-enquiry-to-active-subscription) or [R-01-03](/docs/recipes/membership-lifecycle-recipes/#renew-a-member-manually-and-confirm-the-new-paid-through-date) | Membership option, price, dates, and approval point are visible. |
| 3. Confirm payment or invoice posture | [R-06-01](/docs/recipes/finance-billing-and-commerce-recipes/#take-a-staff-assisted-payment-on-an-open-invoice), [R-06-07](/docs/recipes/finance-billing-and-commerce-recipes/#investigate-a-billing-dispute-with-evidence) | Finance knows whether money is due, paid, disputed, or scheduled. |
| 4. Check access and welcome message | [R-01-08](/docs/recipes/membership-lifecycle-recipes/#help-a-member-regain-portal-access), [R-05-02](/docs/recipes/communications-and-engagement-recipes/#draft-and-preview-a-templated-email-for-one-test-recipient) | The member can be welcomed with the right portal/access guidance. |

**Where you stay in control:** Staff approve the member setup, any payment handling, and any outgoing welcome message.
