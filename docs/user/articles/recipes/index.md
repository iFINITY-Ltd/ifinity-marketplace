A :term[recipe] is a ready-made :term[request] for a common job in iMIS, such as renewing a member, checking an event before registration opens, or finding duplicate contacts. There are 134 recipes, grouped by :term[work area]. Each one says when it helps, what AgentZ will do, what to have ready, and where you stay in control.

:::tip
New to AgentZ? Start with [Making your first request](/docs/getting-started/making-your-first-request/), then [Asking AgentZ to do iMIS work](/docs/ai-apps/what-to-ask/).
:::

## Using a recipe

Do the following to use a recipe:

1. Find the recipe for your job in the work areas below.
2. Read **Before you begin**, and have those details ready.
3. Copy the text after **Ask:** into your AI app.
4. Replace each part in square brackets, such as [member name or ID], with your own details, and send it.
5. Read what AgentZ found, and approve any change it proposes, or say what to change. See [How work is shown and approved](/docs/ai-apps/approvals/).

Each recipe also says what kind of request it is:

- **Look up**: AgentZ reads iMIS and answers. Nothing changes.
- **Review then change**: AgentZ gathers the facts and proposes a change, then waits for your approval.
- **Guided handoff**: part of the job is done in iMIS's own screens, and AgentZ gives you the steps or does them in the :term[iMIS browser].
- **Multi-step plan**: a bigger piece of work that AgentZ plans first, then works through with you.

:::faq
**Do I have to use the recipe's exact words?** No. Change anything to fit your job. The recipe is a starting point.

**Does a recipe change anything by itself?** No. A recipe is only a request. AgentZ still shows you a preview before any change that needs your approval.

**Can I save my own recipes?** No. Keep the requests that work for you in your own documents, and paste them into your AI app when you need them.
:::
