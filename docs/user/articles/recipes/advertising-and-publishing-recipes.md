Prompts for publication advertising setup, inventory, orders, advertiser briefs, revenue reporting, and sales assignments. They connect sales, production, and finance without overpromising inventory or revenue state.

Copy a request into your AI app and replace the words in square brackets with your own. See [Using a recipe](/docs/recipes/#using-a-recipe).

## Review advertising setup and rate cards

**Recipe R-15-01** · **Kind of request:** Look up · **For:** Advertising team, Finance team

:::example
**Ask:** "Please review advertising setup for [publication/channel]. Show publications, ad sizes or placements, rate cards, active dates, finance coding clues, and anything that looks missing or inconsistent. Do not create or change ad records."
:::

**When to use it:** Use this before selling ads, updating a media pack, or reconciling publication setup. Advertising staff need to know what can actually be sold and at what rate.

**What AgentZ does:** AgentZ reads advertising setup records and summarises the commercial structure. It should flag missing rates, inactive items, and setup that needs finance or publication-owner review.

**Before you begin:** Have the publication, issue, channel, or rate-card scope ready.

**Keep in mind:** Rate-card setup affects revenue and contracts. Confirm with the advertising owner before changes.

## Add or update an advertising media asset or ad size

**Recipe R-15-02** · **Kind of request:** Review then change · **For:** Advertising team, Publication manager

:::example
**Ask:** "Please prepare setup for [new publication/ad size/placement]. Check existing media assets, sizes, naming, availability dates, rate-card links, production specs, and finance coding. Show the proposed setup before creating or updating anything."
:::

**When to use it:** Use this when a publication adds a new issue, placement, digital slot, or ad size. Staff need setup that sales, production, and finance can all understand.

**What AgentZ does:** AgentZ reviews existing advertising structures, prepares the new or updated asset details, and waits for approval before supported changes. It should read back the setup after approval.

**Before you begin:** Have name, publication, size/spec, dates, rate, finance coding, and production owner ready.

**Keep in mind:** Ad setup should match the media pack and invoice rules. Avoid informal names that will confuse sales reports.

## Check availability before booking an ad placement

**Recipe R-15-03** · **Kind of request:** Look up · **For:** Advertising sales, Publication manager

:::example
**Ask:** "Please check inventory for [publication/issue/placement/date]. Show what is available, already booked, reserved, conflicting, or unclear. Include advertiser/order references where visible and recommend whether [advertiser] can be offered the placement. Do not book anything yet."
:::

**When to use it:** Use this before confirming an ad sale or promising a placement. Sales staff need a quick answer that does not double-book inventory.

**What AgentZ does:** AgentZ reads advertising orders, placements, and inventory evidence, then gives an availability recommendation with caveats. It should keep the task read-only until booking is approved.

**Before you begin:** Have publication, issue/date, placement/size, and advertiser request ready.

**Keep in mind:** Inventory may also be tracked in production calendars. Confirm high-value placements with the publication owner.

## Book an advertising order after approval

**Recipe R-15-04** · **Kind of request:** Review then change · **For:** Advertising sales, Finance team

:::example
**Ask:** "Please prepare an ad order for [advertiser] in [publication/issue] for [placement/size] at [rate/discount]. Check advertiser record, inventory, rate card, billing details, sales rep, production due date, and conflicts. Show the order summary and required confirmation before booking."
:::

**When to use it:** Use this when an advertiser has agreed to buy a placement. Staff need the order, billing, and production details correct before it becomes a finance or fulfilment commitment.

**What AgentZ does:** AgentZ confirms the advertiser, availability, pricing, and order details, then prepares the booking for approval. It should read back the order and line details after an approved create/update.

**Before you begin:** Have advertiser, publication/issue, placement, rate, discount approval, sales rep, and production notes ready.

**Keep in mind:** Advertising orders create commercial commitments. Confirm price and availability before booking.

## Review an advertiser account and current orders

**Recipe R-15-05** · **Kind of request:** Look up · **For:** Advertising sales, Finance team

:::example
**Ask:** "Please prepare an advertiser account brief for [advertiser]. Include order history, current bookings, outstanding balances, production status, contacts, sales rep, recent communications if visible, and renewal or upsell opportunities. Do not change orders."
:::

**When to use it:** Use this before a sales call, renewal conversation, production chase, or finance query. The advertising team needs the full commercial context.

**What AgentZ does:** AgentZ pulls together advertiser, order, finance, and production evidence into a concise account brief. It should separate confirmed facts from suggested follow-up.

**Before you begin:** Have advertiser name/ID and the purpose of the review ready.

**Keep in mind:** Production details may live outside iMIS. Note any external checks needed.

## Report advertising revenue by publication

**Recipe R-15-06** · **Kind of request:** Multi-step plan · **For:** Advertising team, Finance team, Leadership team

:::example
**Ask:** "Please prepare advertising revenue posture for [period]. Group orders by publication, issue, placement, advertiser, sales rep, booked amount, invoiced amount, paid amount, and outstanding balance. Explain source and exclusions before creating any reusable report."
:::

**When to use it:** Use this for sales meetings, finance reconciliation, or publication performance review. Teams need to distinguish booked revenue from invoiced and paid revenue.

**What AgentZ does:** AgentZ reviews advertising order and finance evidence, proposes revenue definitions, and prepares a reusable report after approval. It should make differences between booked, invoiced, and paid explicit.

**Before you begin:** Have reporting period, publications, revenue definitions, and audience ready.

**Keep in mind:** Revenue language matters. Agree whether the report is sales pipeline, invoiced revenue, or cash received.

## Review or update sales rep and territory assignment

**Recipe R-15-07** · **Kind of request:** Review then change · **For:** Advertising manager, Sales operations

:::example
**Ask:** "Please review sales assignment for [advertiser/order/territory]. Show current rep, territory, order history, open opportunities or bookings, commission/reporting impact if visible, and the proposed change to [new rep/territory]. Wait for approval before updating records."
:::

**When to use it:** Use this when accounts move between reps, territories change, or an order needs the right owner for reporting. Staff need to avoid breaking sales-credit reports.

**What AgentZ does:** AgentZ reads advertiser/order assignment evidence, explains the reporting impact, and prepares the update for approval. It should verify the new assignment after an approved change.

**Before you begin:** Have advertiser/order, current and new rep/territory, effective date, and manager approval ready.

**Keep in mind:** Sales assignment can affect reports and commission processes. Confirm the effective date and scope.
