## Gifts, pledges, receipts and Gift Aid

AgentZ records gifts and pledges, issues receipts, manages campaigns and appeals, and estimates Gift Aid claims for you to review.

**What AgentZ does:**

- AgentZ gives you a donor's giving history, pledges, Gift Aid status and the communications they have received.
- AgentZ creates and changes campaigns, appeals, source codes and solicitations.
- AgentZ records and posts cash gifts, including soft credits and tributes, and takes one-off card gifts through the secure Pay Central payment form.
- AgentZ creates pledge instalment schedules and records each instalment payment.
- AgentZ prepares the link to your own recurring donation page, and checks the donor's sign-up once they have completed it.
- AgentZ issues a receipt for a gift or sends it by email through iMIS, and checks whether it was delivered.
- AgentZ changes the date of a receipted gift, and voids and reissues a receipt.
- AgentZ reads a donor's Gift Aid declarations from iMIS, with each one's status, and estimates a tax year's Gift Aid claim from the gifts iMIS lists as claimable.
- AgentZ sets up premiums and tribute types.
- AgentZ tells you what your **Settings > Fundraising > General** values are.

**Needs your approval:**

- Before AgentZ records gifts, receipts or acknowledgements, or makes any other change to a donor's financial records, it asks you to approve it.
- Before AgentZ changes campaign, appeal or premium setup, or deletes a receipt, it asks you to approve it.

**What AgentZ does not do:**

- AgentZ cannot submit Gift Aid claims to HMRC; iMIS submits them from **Fundraising > Gift Aid > Process Claims**.
- AgentZ cannot record, change or cancel a Gift Aid declaration yet; record it on the donor's **Giving** tab under **Gift Aid > Declarations**, and AgentZ reads it back.
- AgentZ does not count a row in a custom Gift Aid panel your organisation added as a Gift Aid declaration.
- AgentZ cannot set up a recurring gift unless the donor completes the secure Pay Central page in iMIS.
- AgentZ cannot download or regenerate receipt PDFs, or resend a receipt that has already been sent.
- AgentZ cannot edit a receipted gift freely; it changes only the gift date.
- AgentZ cannot change your **Settings > Fundraising > General** values yet.

## Examples

:::example
**Ask:** "Show me donor 12345's giving over the last three years, their open pledges and their Gift Aid status."

You see the donor's gifts by year, pledges with what is still to pay, and each Gift Aid declaration with its status.
:::

:::example
**Ask:** "Record a cash gift of £500 from Jane Smith to the Spring Appeal, and email her a receipt."

You see a preview of the gift and the receipt, and approve it. AgentZ records the gift, sends the receipt through iMIS and tells you whether it was delivered.
:::

:::faq
**Can AgentZ submit our Gift Aid claim?** No. iMIS submits claims to HMRC from **Fundraising > Gift Aid > Process Claims**. AgentZ estimates the claim for you to check first.
:::
