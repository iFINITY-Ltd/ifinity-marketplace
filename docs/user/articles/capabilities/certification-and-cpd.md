AgentZ sets up certification programmes, enrols members in them and tells you how far each member has got.

## Certification programmes and enrolments

AgentZ sets up certification programmes, enrols members in them, and reads a member's certification progress.

**What AgentZ does:**

- AgentZ creates, changes and removes certification programmes, their components and groups, experience locations, and unit types such as CPD, CEUs and hours.
- AgentZ enrols a member in a certification programme and checks the enrolment.
- AgentZ reads a member's certification record and their progress through a programme.
- AgentZ lists and searches your certification programmes.
- AgentZ tells you what your certification settings are, such as grace periods.

**Needs your approval:**

- Before AgentZ enrols members or changes certification setup, including unit types, it asks you to approve it.

**What AgentZ does not do:**

- AgentZ cannot record a member's CPD, CPE or experience credit; record it in iMIS, and AgentZ reads it back.
- AgentZ cannot set up programme rules, or change transcript and credential pages; do those in iMIS.
- AgentZ cannot award credits automatically when a member attends an event.
- AgentZ cannot change your certification settings yet.

## Examples

:::example
**Ask:** "How far has member 12345 got with the Professional Certificate? Show what they still need."

You see the member's record for the programme and their progress, read from iMIS.
:::

:::example
**Ask:** "Enrol Jane Smith, ID 12345, in the Professional Certificate programme."

You see a preview of the enrolment and approve it. AgentZ enrols her and checks the enrolment.
:::

:::faq
**Can AgentZ record CPD hours for a member?** No, not yet. Record them in iMIS, and AgentZ reads them back.
:::
